Privacy Notice

What we collect

Your name, phone number, optional email address, the reason for your visit, a photo taken at check-in, your electronic signature, and the geographic location at which you signed this form.

Why we collect the location

The location is recorded once, at the moment you sign, as part of the signed record. It establishes where the form was completed. It is not used to track you, and it is not collected at any other time.

How it is kept

The signed record is stored in the private files of Ability and Empowerment Services and is treated as protected health information under the HIPAA Privacy Rule. It is never sold, and never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your record at any time by contacting the practice at (443) 438-5538.
